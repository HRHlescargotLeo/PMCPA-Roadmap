Headlines instead of "Click here"

Show what each news item is about, and make the headline the link.

High impactSmall changeAlso part of prototype 5: Open by default

Proposed

Latest news

  • 25 September 2026Eli Lilly ruled in breach of Clause 2 (CASE/0685/08/25)
  • 24 September 2026[Company] ruled in breach of Clause 2 ([case reference])
  • [Date]Consultation on changes to the Constitution and Procedure: now closed

What we saw

On the Media page, each news item shows "Click here", a date and "read more", with no headline. The homepage heading reads "Latest PMCPA News - click tiles below", and each tile's link says "Click here".

What changes

Show the headline and date for every item, make the headline the link, and remove "click" from headings and buttons.

The 25 September headline uses the facts in that press release. We didn't open the 24 September release, so its headline is a placeholder.
