Clause lists in clause order

Let people find a clause the way they think of it: 14.2 comes after 14.1.

High impactSmall changeAlso part of prototype 3: The Code

Today on pmcpa.org.uk

SEARCH BY CLAUSE

Proposed

Choose a clause from the list, such as 26.2

What we saw

The "Search by clause" list on Q&As by topic or clause runs "Clause 10.1, 12.1, 15.1, 24.1, 26.1, 28.1, 31.1, 8.1, 12.2, 15.2…", ordered by sub-clause number rather than clause.

What changes

Numeric order, grouped under the six Code sections, plus a box that accepts a clause number such as 14.2.

The proposed list shows the clauses the live Q&A list offers, sorted and grouped by section.
