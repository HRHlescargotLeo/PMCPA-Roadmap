Media

SCROLL DOWN FOR THE LATEST PRESS RELEASES For media enquiries about any aspect of the work of the PMCPA please contact us via the switchboard…

The PMCPA publishes certain Sanctions (advertisements, public reprimands and corrective statements) Press Releases and Case Reports. In each case where a breach of the ABPI Code of Practice for the Pharmaceutical Industry is ruled, the company concerned must give an undertaking…

Additional sanctions can also be imposed. These include: the carrying out by the PMCPA of an audit of a company's procedures… requiring the company to issue a corrective statement… a public reprimand… suspension or expulsion from membership of the ABPI…

Case Reports: When cases are considered under the Code they will first appear on this site as an ongoing case…
