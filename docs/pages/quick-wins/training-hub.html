Today on pmcpa.org.uk

Training on the Code

The Code requires that all relevant personnel, including those employed through third parties, must be trained on the Code.

  • PMCPA learning portal
  • ABPI Code training – four dates available to book
  • Application for an extension of time in connection with the ABPI Advanced Programme for Industry Personnel (previously called ABPI Medical Representatives Examination) and the ABPI Intermediate Programme for Industry Personnel (previously called ABPI Generic Sales Representatives Examination)
  • NOMINATED SIGNATORIES FORM

What we saw

The Training page is four links side by side: the learning portal, the seminar dates, a 300-character title about exam extensions, and "NOMINATED SIGNATORIES FORM".

What changes

Four short cards by need (learn the Code, book a seminar, exam extensions, nominated signatories), each with its format, length and price.
