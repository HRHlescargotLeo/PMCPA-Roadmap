A one-line summary for every case

Say what the case was about and what was decided, in a sentence anyone can follow.

Medium impactSmall changeAlso part of prototype 1: Case finder

Today on pmcpa.org.uk

  • AUTH/2949/3/17 - Hospital doctor v A MenariniYellow Card Scheme details missing from company website

    A hospital doctor complained after he had accessed the A. Menarini corporate website to find out more about, and report an adverse event to, one of the company's medicines. The complainant submitted that a number of links on the website did not work including one promising 'more information on medicines licensed in the UK'. There were no adverse event reporting ...

Proposed

  1. AUTH/2949/3/17Breach and no breach

    Yellow Card Scheme details missing from company website

    Hospital doctor v A Menarini

    A company website had broken links and no Yellow Card reporting details; the Panel ruled breaches of Clauses 9.1 and 26.3.

    Breach9.126.3No breach4.9

    2016 Code · Completed 7 June 2017

What we saw

Results show a subtitle and then the first 400 or so characters of the case summary, cut off mid-sentence. The outcome isn't in the text you see.

What changes

Keep the subtitle as the headline, and add one sentence written for a non-specialist: what was complained about and what the Panel decided. It can be drafted when the case report is approved.

Our one-line summary is drawn from the published case summary and ruling. In practice the PMCPA would write and approve these.
