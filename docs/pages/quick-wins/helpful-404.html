A "page not found" that helps

Help people who follow an old link find what they were after.

High impactSmall change

Today on pmcpa.org.uk

Page Not Found

Proposed

We can't find that page

It may have moved when the 2024 Code came in. Try searching, or use one of these.

  • Completed cases
  • The 2024 Code
  • Make a complaint
  • Q&As
  • Contact us

What we saw

A missing page on pmcpa.org.uk shows "Page Not Found" and nothing else: no header, footer, search or links.

What changes

Keep the normal header and footer, add a search box, links to the most-used pages and a contact link. A monthly broken-link report catches old links to fix or redirect.
