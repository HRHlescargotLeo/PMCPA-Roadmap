All quick wins Quick win 28 of 29 · Housekeeping

Lighter font loading

Stop loading fonts the site doesn't use, so pages appear sooner.

Low impactSmall change

Today on pmcpa.org.uk

  • fonts.googleapis.com · Poppins 400, 400 italic, 600, 600 italic, 700, 700 italic
  • gilroy.otf
  • GilroyThin.woff
  • GilroySemiBold.woff
  • GilroyBold.woff
  • icomoon.ttf / .woff
  • meno-banner (Adobe Fonts)

Proposed

  • Poppins × 6 (not used)
  • gilroy.woff2 preloaded
  • gilroy-thin.woff2
  • gilroy-semibold.woff2
  • gilroy-bold.woff2
  • icomoon.woff2
  • meno-banner (Adobe Fonts) preloaded

What we saw

The homepage requests Poppins in six styles from Google Fonts, which we didn't find in use. Gilroy is served as an OpenType (OTF) file rather than the smaller WOFF2.

What changes

Remove Poppins, serve Gilroy as WOFF2, and preload the two faces used at the top of the page.
