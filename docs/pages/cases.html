Prototype 1 of 5 · Case finder

What we're proposing

A completed-cases search that opens on the latest rulings and lets you narrow by the things people actually look for: the clause, the outcome, the company, who complained, the Code year, sanctions and when the case finished. Each result says what happened in one line, shows the outcome at a glance, and links its clauses to the Code. You can save any search as an email alert.

Why

Today Completed cases opens with no sort selected, so the first results we saw were completed in 2017 and 2018, out of 1,923. The only filters are year received and Code year, even though clauses, outcome, complainant and sanctions are already recorded on every case. On a phone the first result is about two screens down, below the introduction and the search tips.

Case data here is invented (fictional companies, CASE/9xxx references). The filters use fields the case pages already carry.

Cases

Completed cases

Every case ruled under the Code, breach or not, with the newest first.

How to search
  • Type a case number in either format, AUTH/ or CASE/, with or without the slashes.
  • Put a phrase in quotation marks to match it exactly: "data on file".
  • Use the filters to narrow by clause, outcome, company, complainant, Code year, sanctions or date.
BehaviourSearch matches the reference, parties, headline and summary. "9071", "case 9071" and "CASE/9071/03/26" all find the same case. The search terms and filters are kept in the address, so a search can be shared or bookmarked.
