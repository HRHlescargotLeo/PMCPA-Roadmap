Prototype 4 of 5 · Raise a concern

What we're proposing

One journey from "is this for the PMCPA?" to a sent complaint. A short scope check comes first and points anything outside the Code to the right body. Its answers carry into a stepped form: the matter, evidence, about you, your interests, then a check-and-send screen. The guidance sits beside the field it applies to, and anonymity is explained in one small table. When you send, you get a reference and a timeline of what happens next.

Why

Today the scope check is a Typeform on a separate page and nothing carries over to the form. The form follows roughly 1,000 words of guidance, has a single page of fields, and ends with "If you cannot submit the form you may need to clear your Cache". One sentence says you will have means of appeal after a no-breach ruling; another says you won't. Nothing tells a complainant what happens next or roughly how long it takes.

Nothing is sent. The wording follows the live Make a complaint page, with the appeal sentence corrected.

What we can look at

We apply the ABPI Code of Practice to pharmaceutical companies: how they promote prescription medicines in the UK and how they work with health professionals, patient organisations and the public.

We can't advise on treatment, look into individual professionals, or deal with side effects. We'll point you to who can.

Question for the PMCPAIs this the right set of first questions, and the right bodies to refer to? The live Typeform's logic would be the starting point.

Help us deal with it

The PMCPA isn't an investigatory body. Complaints are judged on the evidence the parties provide, and you need to show your complaint is more likely than not to be right.

Say exactly what was said or done, by whom, where and when. If you know which clause of the Code applies, mention it, but you don't have to.
