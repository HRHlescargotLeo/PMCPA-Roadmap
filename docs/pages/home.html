Prototype 5 of 5 · Open by default: homepage

What we're proposing

A homepage that sends each visitor the right way in a click: raise a concern, check the Code, search cases or the media centre. Under that, the latest rulings with their outcomes, news with real headlines, training dates with an honest status, and a short view of how the PMCPA is performing, linking to a full performance page and email alerts people can tailor.

Why

Today the homepage has no link to Make a complaint in its main content, and on a phone it is 8,217px tall. The news heading says "click tiles below" and each tile's link reads "Click here". Training is "now available to book" while every 2026 date is fully booked. The PMCPA's progress (55% more rulings in 2025, open cases down from 155 to 65) is only in a news post.

Rulings and news are sample items with invented companies. Training dates and performance figures are as published.
