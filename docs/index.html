Five ideas for pmcpa.org.uk

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the PMCPA website. Each works as it would on the live site, designed to sit with the current PMCPA brand.

Hero image

The prototypes

  1. Case finder
    1

    Find the right precedent quickly

    Completed cases open on the latest rulings, with filters for clause, outcome, company, complainant, Code year, sanctions and dates. Every result shows its outcome and clauses at a glance, and any search can become an email alert.

    Try: filter to Clause 14 under Section 2, then add "Breach ruled" and sort by oldest first.

    Open prototype
  2. Case report
    2

    A case report you can read on screen

    Outcome, dates and sanctions at the top, a ruling for each clause linked to the Code, a timeline, the full report in sections, and related cases, with copy link, citation and print.

    Try: select Clause 3.1 in the rulings table, then come back and open a related case.

    Open prototype
  3. The Code
    3

    The Code, connected to its Q&As, guidance and cases

    One search across all four, sections that open in place, "go to 14.2", and a clause page where every sub-clause shows its Q&As, guidance and cases.

    Try: search "data on file", switch between the tabs, then open Clause 14.3 and select "health professional".

    Open prototype
  4. Raise a concern
    4

    Raise a concern, step by step

    A scope check that points anything outside the Code elsewhere, then a stepped form with guidance beside each field, anonymity explained in a table, a check-and-send screen and a timeline of what happens next.

    Try: choose "Side effects of a medicine" first, then switch to "How a company promotes a medicine" and complete the form.

    Open prototype
  5. Open by default
    5

    Open by default

    A homepage that routes each visitor, the latest rulings and real headlines, honest training status, an Our performance page with ongoing cases you can follow, and email alerts you can tailor.

    Try: on Our performance, switch the snapshot to 30 June 2026; on Email alerts, pick Section 2 and watch the sample alert change.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: search, filters, sorting, alerts, tabs, the clause hub, the stepped form and its validation. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for the PMCPA (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Case data is invented: the companies are fictional and references use CASE/9xxx and AUTH/9xxx, outside the PMCPA's real numbering. Code clause titles, Clause 14's text, Q&A and guidance titles, training dates and performance figures are as published on pmcpa.org.uk.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts, especially the case filters and the form.
