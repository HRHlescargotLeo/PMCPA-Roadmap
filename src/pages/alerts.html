What we're proposing

An on-site preference centre for eAlerts. Choose what you want (new cases, sanctions, Q&As and guidance, training, press releases), narrow it to Code sections or companies, and pick how often. A sample alert updates as you choose, so you know what you'll get before you sign up.

Why

The eAlert link in the header goes to an off-site HubSpot form, and nothing on the site says what the alerts contain or how often they come. Compliance teams follow particular clauses and competitors; the alerts could do that for them.
