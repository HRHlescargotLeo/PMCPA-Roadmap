What we saw

Advertised sanctions lists 239 entries. An advertisement and a public reprimand for the same case appear as separate entries, and titles vary ("Advert - Sanofi - SANOFI – CASE/0688/08/25").

What changes

One entry per case, with each sanction as a label, a consistent title, the date posted and a link to the case report.

Entries are as published on Advertised sanctions on 7 October 2026. Links would open each case report.
