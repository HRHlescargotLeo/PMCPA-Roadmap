Make releases findable by company and case, and read well when shared.

What we saw

Press releases are titled "PMCPA PRESS RELEASE - 25 September 2026 - Case ruled in breach of Clause 2". The company is only in the body, and the page has no meta description.

What changes

Put the company and case in the title, use the release's first sentence as its description, and add a share image.
