What we saw

Results list "Breach Clause(s)" and "No Breach Clause(s)" as plain text. AUTH/3048/6/18 lists 7.2 and 7.3 under both, which reads as a contradiction unless you open the report.

What changes

Add an outcome label to every result (Breach ruled, No breach, Breach and no breach) and show clauses as chips, solid for a breach and outlined for no breach. 2024 Code clauses carry their section colour and link to the clause.
