Proposed

    What we saw

    Q&As by topic or clause has two dropdowns and no keyword box. The page says "Q&As were last revised in October 2024, unless a later date is stated", but the dates aren't shown on each one.

    What changes

    A keyword box that filters as you type, the revision date on each Q&A, and "Updated" markers when one changes.
