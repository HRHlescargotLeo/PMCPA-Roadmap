    What we saw

    The only date filter on Completed cases is Year received (2026, 2025, 2024 and so on). The date most people mean by "recent rulings" is when the case was completed, and that isn't a filter.

    What changes

    Add a Completed filter with presets (last 6 months, last 12 months, last 2 years) and a custom date range, with a count beside each option.
