What we saw

The homepage requests Poppins in six styles from Google Fonts, which we didn't find in use. Gilroy is served as an OpenType (OTF) file rather than the smaller WOFF2.

What changes

Remove Poppins, serve Gilroy as WOFF2, and preload the two faces used at the top of the page.
