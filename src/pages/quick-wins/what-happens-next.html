What we saw

The Make a complaint form ends with reCAPTCHA and "If you cannot submit the form you may need to clear your Cache". Nothing on the page sets out the stages or typical timings.

What changes

A confirmation screen with a reference number and a short timeline using the stages the PMCPA already reports in its ongoing-cases table, with typical timings the PMCPA is happy to publish.
