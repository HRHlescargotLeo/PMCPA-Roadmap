Fix the appeal sentence

Correct a sentence that tells complainants the opposite of the rest of the page.

High impactSmall changeAlso part of prototype 4: Raise a concern

Today on pmcpa.org.uk

If you become uncontactable, you will be unable to participate in the process fully. … If the Panel rules no breach of the Code, the case will be closed – you will have no means of appeal and will not have the opportunity to see the proposed text of the case report before it is published.

Complaints may also be submitted via the post to: Chief Executive, … If you choose not to provide any contact details, you will be unable to participate in the process fully. … If the Panel rules no breach of the Code, you will have means of appeal.

Proposed

Use one you check regularly. If we can't reach you and the Panel rules no breach, the case will be closed and you won't be able to appeal.

What we saw

On Make a complaint, the anonymity section says that if the Panel rules no breach "you will have no means of appeal". The paragraph about posting a complaint says "If the Panel rules no breach of the Code, you will have means of appeal."

What changes

Correct the postal sentence (we assume a missing "no") and say it once, beside the email field it depends on.
