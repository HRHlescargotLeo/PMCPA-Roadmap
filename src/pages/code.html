Prototype 3 of 5 · The Code, connected

What we're proposing

One place to check the Code. A single search covers the Code, Q&As, guidance and cases, with a tab for each and no duplicates. Sections open in place and each has its own address. You can jump straight to a clause by typing "14.2". On every clause page, the Q&As, guidance and cases for each sub-clause sit beside its text.

Why

Today the Code, Q&As, guidance and cases are four separate places. A Code search for "social media" returned 71 Code-only results with Clause 26 listed four times, and the social media guidance wasn't among them. The Q&A clause list runs 10.1, 12.1, 15.1… 8.1, 12.2, and the Code page title still says "Comes into operation 1 October 2024".

Clause titles, Clause 14's text and the Q&A and guidance titles are as published. Which clauses each Q&A and guidance item is tagged to is our illustration.
