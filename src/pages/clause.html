Prototype 3 of 5 · Clause page

What we're proposing

Every clause as a hub. Each sub-clause shows its text and supplementary information, then the Q&As, guidance and cases that apply to it, each panel appearing only when there's something in it, with "See all cases under 14.2" opening the case finder already filtered. Q&As open in place. Terms defined in Clause 1 show their definition where they're used.

Why

The live clause pages already show recent cases under each sub-clause, which is a strong start. But "See all" goes to the general cases page, and the Q&As and guidance for the same clause are on other pages with no link between them.

All 31 clauses show their published text and supplementary information, the Q&As tagged to them and the most recent cases listed under them on pmcpa.org.uk (read 6 November 2026). Guidance tags are ours where the guidance page doesn't give one. Open another clause from the index on the left.
