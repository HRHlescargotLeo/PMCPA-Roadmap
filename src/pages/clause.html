What we're proposing

Every clause as a hub. Each sub-clause shows its text and supplementary information, then the Q&As, guidance and cases that apply to it, with "See all cases under 14.4" opening the case finder already filtered. Terms defined in Clause 1 show their definition in place.

Why

The live clause pages already show recent cases under each sub-clause, which is a strong start. But "See all" goes to the general cases page, and the Q&As and guidance for the same clause are on other pages with no link between them.

Clause 14 shows its published text. Other clauses show their title with placeholder text. Open another clause from the index on the left.
