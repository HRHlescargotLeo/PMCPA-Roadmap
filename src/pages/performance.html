What we're proposing

A page that shows how the complaints procedure is performing, updated each month from the same figures as the ongoing-cases table: rulings by year, open cases over time, how many Panel rulings stand, and how many cases are at each stage now compared with the last snapshot. Below it, ongoing cases with a simple status track and a "follow" option.

Why

The PMCPA has a good story to tell (161 rulings in 2025 against 104 in 2024, open cases down from 155 to 65 by June 2026, 98% of Panel rulings accepted or upheld), but it lives in one news post. The ongoing-cases table has two unlabelled total rows and refers to "the table on the right".

All figures in the charts and table are as published by the PMCPA. The ongoing cases listed underneath are invented.
